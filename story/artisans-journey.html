About this story

The story follows a weaver at work and then steps back to show the context around the craft: family stories, sourcing materials, and the effort of keeping traditional knowledge useful in the present.

Its slower pacing invites the viewer to notice repetition, rhythm, and care as meaningful parts of the narrative rather than filler between plot points.
