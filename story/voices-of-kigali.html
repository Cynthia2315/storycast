About this story

Voices of Kigali looks at the city after dark, when illuminated buildings, movement, and public spaces begin to shape the mood of the night.

The piece is structured like a slow visual walk through the skyline: short scenes, careful pauses, and a calm rhythm that gives the city room to breathe.
